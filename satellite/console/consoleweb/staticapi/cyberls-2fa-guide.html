How to setup the 2-FA ?

Enable 2-FA (Two Factor Authentication)

  1. Open the browser and enter https://cyberls.com/.
  1. Click on Connect with google Button.
  1. Continue with the google authentication process and then finally you will be logged in to the Cyberls. The Dashboard View will confirm you have Logged in to the CyberLS.
  1. Now click on the settings you will be redirected to the setting page.
  1. Find the Button Enable 2-FA and click on that.
  1. Scan the Code using the Google Authenticator and enter the 6 digit code. Finally you will get the Recovery code download and save the code. As shown below.
  1. Now you have saved the Codes you can see the setting page is updated saying the 2-FA is enabled and is Active.

Disable 2-FA (Two Factor Authentication)

  1. In the setting page click on the Disable 2-FA button. Fill in the Google Authentication Code and Confirm.
  1. The below slide confirms the 2-FA is Disabled.
