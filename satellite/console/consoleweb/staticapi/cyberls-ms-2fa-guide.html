Enable 2FA in the CyberLS

  1. Navigate to the cyberLs Homepage using the URL “https://outh365.cyberls.com/”
  1. Click on the Login button in the Homepage or Use the link to navigate to the Login page. “https://outh365.cyberls.com/login”.
  1. Fill in the CyberLs User credentials your Microsoft mail id and the password associated with the cyberLs used while trying to signup into the CyberLs to Login.
  2. Once Logged in using the Email and the password you will be taken to the Dashboard as shown below.
  1. Click on the setting option in the sidebar.You will be able to see the below UI.
  1. Once you have navigated to the setting section find the “Enable 2FA” button and click on it.
  1. Scan the Qr Code using the Google Authenticator or Microsoft authenticator and enter the code on the Input field and click on the proceed button.
  1. You will be asked to download the recovery Codes Download it and save it.Once done now click on “Close and done” button.
  2. Now you will see the 2FA enabled button updated to “Disable 2FA app”. Confirming the 2 FA is enabled.

Disable 2FA in the CyberLS

  1. Navigate to the cyberLs Homepage using the URL “https://outh365.cyberls.com/”
  1. Click on the Login button in the Homepage or Use the link to navigate to the Login page. “https://outh365.cyberls.com/login”.
  1. Fill in the CyberLs User credentials your Microsoft mail id and the password associated with the cyberLs used while trying to signup into the CyberLs to Login.
  2. Once Logged in using the Email and the password you will be taken to the Dashboard as shown below.
  1. Click on the setting option in the sidebar.You will be able to see the below UI.
  1. Locate the “Disable 2FA App” button and Click on the “Disable 2FA App” button.You will be asked to enter the Code for the Confirmation and click on the “Disable 2FA”.
  1. The below UI Confirms the 2FA is disabled Successfully.
