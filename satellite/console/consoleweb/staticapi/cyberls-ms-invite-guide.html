Invite User and Shared Services

  1. Navigate to the cyberLs Homepage using the URL “https://outh365.cyberls.com/”
  1. Click on the Login button in the Homepage or Use the link to navigate to the Login page. “https://outh365.cyberls.com/connect”.
  1. Click on the “Continue with Microsoft” button and you will be asked to authenticate using the Microsoft account as shown below
  1. Once the account is authenticated successfully you will be redirected to the Dashboard page as shown below. Confirming the Login was successful.
  1. Now from the SideBar select the User and Groups and Navigate to it.
  1. Select the account which needs to be Invited, Review the services that need to be shared and update the permission related to the access for the Restore, download and Preview of the mail data and click on the Invite Button form the below view.
  1. Once the Invitation link is sent and updated then the invited user will get a mail of the Invitation to preview his microsoft services Backed up by the CyberLs.

Now lets see how the Invited user can preview the Invitation and accept and preview the Shared Services access.

  1. Go to the Invited user outlook mailbox and check for the Cyberls Invitation mail. A link will be found, click on the link and then you will be asked to Signup and then taken to the Team and Invite section. As shown below.
  1. If you are an already existing user and have got the Invitation to check the Invitation go to the Team and invites section.
  1. Click on the Accept button. As shown below now you can preview the services.
  1. Now go to the User and Groups section and find the “Shared Services” button click on that and the list will appear as shown below.
  1. Click on the View Data to preview the Services as shared with you.
