Corporate User (Admin) Signup Guide and the Onboarding process.

  1. Navigate to the cyberLs Homepage using the URL “https://outh365.cyberls.com/”
  1. Click on the “Signup free trial” button in the Homepage or Use the link to navigate to the Signup page. “https://outh365.cyberls.com/connect”.
  1. Click on the “Continue with Microsoft” you will be asked to authenticate via microsoft as shown below.
  1. Once the authentication process and the access to the services is given then you will be asked to select the Organization account or Self account. Select Organization account.
  1. Click on the “Connect and Continue” button and next you will be asked to select the Entire organization or specific mail boxes, select the Services that needs to be backed up (Outlook, Onedrive, Contacts, Calendar) as shown below.
  1. Scroll down to set up the Backup frequency and the Storage Destination (By default Cyberls network).
  1. Once the form is filled click on the “Check access and activate” button now you will be asked to review the services that are available for the backup by Microsoft. As shown below.
  1. Review the services and click on the “Continue and create Jobs” button. Now you will be taken to the Dashboard Confirming the Signup to Cyberls Network.
  1. Now click on the User and Group in the sidebar and now you will be able to review the accounts added to the Cyberls network as shown below.
