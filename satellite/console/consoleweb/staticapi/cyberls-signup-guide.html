How to signup in the CyberLs ?

Signup to CyberLs

  1. Open the browser and enter https://cyberls.com/.
  2. Now in the header click on the Start free trial.
  1. Click on the Button “Connect with Google”.
  1. Once you have authenticated and provided the access to the google services.
  1. Setup your Password then you will be taken to the Next Onboarding step asking for the Service selection as shown below.
  1. Once you have set up the Services now you will be taken to the next Onboarding step “Auto-Sync”.
  1. Once you have selected the Configuration Auto-Sync, You will proceed with the next Onboarding step asking for the “Go to Dashboard”.
  1. Click on the “Go to Dashboard” You will be taken to the CyberLS Dashboard. Congratulation’s you have finally Created your account.
