How to login CyberLS ?

Login to CyberLs using the Google Authentication Process.

  1. Click on the Login button https://cyberls.com/.
  1. Click on Connect with google Button.
  1. Continue with the google authentication process and then finally you will be logged in to the Cyberls. The Dashboard View will confirm you have Logged in to the CyberLS.

Login to CyberLs using the Email & Password.

  1. Click on the Login button https://cyberls.com/.
  1. Click on Login with Email & Password button.
  1. Fill the Email, Password, Confirm you are not a robot and finally Click on the Login.
  1. The Dashboard View will confirm you have Logged in to the CyberLS.

Login to CyberLs using the Email & Password After user have setup the 2-FA ( Factor Authentication )

  1. Click on the Login button https://cyberls.com/.
  1. Click on Login with Email & Password button.
  1. Fill the Email, Password, Confirm you are not a robot and finally Click on the Login.
  1. Now you will be asked to Authenticate using the MFA. Enter the Code that is present in the Google authenticator or provide the downloaded recovery Code.
  1. Now Click on Verify and complete Login to view into the Dashboard.
