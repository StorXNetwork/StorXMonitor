How to connect google account in the Cyberls ?

Add Corporate Account

  1. Login to the CyberLs https://cyberls.com/, using the google account.
  2. Once you are in the dashboard from the sidebar select “User & Groups”.
  1. Once you are in the “User & Groups” you will find the Connect Account.
  1. Click on the Connect Account you can see the Pop up asking for Google Account authentication.
  1. Once you have added the Corporate account you can see the list of the mails associated with the admin google account. Select the mails you want to start backing up for. Before you proceed with the next step make sure you have followed the setup in the Domain wide delegation.
  1. Click on the Continue button. You will be taken to the Service selection pop up and select the service you want to backup.
  1. You will be directed with the next pop up asking for the policy selection or policy Creation. Create the policy. Click on the continue button.
  1. Now select the Auto sync Intervals and then click on the continue.
  1. Now that you have added the Account successfully you can see the list updated in the User and groups listing page.
