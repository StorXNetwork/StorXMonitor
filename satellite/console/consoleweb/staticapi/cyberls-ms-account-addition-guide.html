Corporate Admin account addition

  1. Navigate to the cyberLs Homepage using the URL “https://outh365.cyberls.com/”
  1. Click on the Login button in the Homepage or Use the link to navigate to the Login page. “https://outh365.cyberls.com/connect”.
  1. Click on the “Continue with Microsoft” button and you will be asked to authenticate using the Microsoft account as shown below
  1. Once the account is authenticated successfully you will be redirected to the Dashboard page as shown below. Confirming the Login was successful.
  1. Now click on the User and group and locate the “Connect account” button and click on it.
  1. Once you click on the Connect Account you will get the pop up asking for the “Connect your Microsoft account” as shown below.
  1. Once the authentication with the microsoft is done successfully then you will get the pop up asking for the selection on just the admin account or the complete organization account. Select any one as per your desired choice and click on the Connect and Continue.
  1. Now you will be asked to setup the policy based on the services (outlook, onedrive, calendar, Contacts) as shown below.
  1. Now you will have to set up the sync frequency and set up the Storage destination (Default location is CyberLs Network).
  1. Once the Services selection is done successfully you will now be able to see the services that is going to be listed based on the account.
  1. Review the services and click on the continue.
  1. Now you will be able to see the list getting updated.
